Process

How a project runs.

01

The brief

A short call or a few emails. You tell me what you sell and what the site needs to do. I come back with a fixed quote so you always know the number.

02

Design preview

I send you a link you can open on your phone and your laptop. We agree on the direction before I build anything.

03

Build and review

I build the real thing, then we review it together on that same link. Changes show up while you watch.

04

Launch and handoff

Domain, hosting, payments, all wired up and tested. Then I walk you through managing it yourself, so you're not calling me for every price change.

About

The short version.

I'm Pranav, a web designer and developer in Ontario. I didn't start with an agency or a bootcamp. I started by building real online stores, which taught me what it takes to get a site from an idea to something people buy from.

Now I do the same for small businesses: clean, fast websites without agency prices or six-week timelines. WordPress when you want to manage content yourself. Hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript when lighter and faster wins.

I also use AI tools for research, content drafts, and testing, which is a big part of how I keep prices where they are.
